Compounding Planner

Compare campaign paths using the published campaign costs, one-time level activations, 12 click days, and seven-day hold. This is a cash-flow model—not a promise, recommendation, or return projection.

Important reality check: the published materials describe campaign purchases as advertising services, not investments. This tool assumes every required daily click is completed, every campaign pays exactly as shown, funds are usable immediately after the stated hold, no deposits/withdrawals/referrals occur, and no fees beyond listed activation fees apply. Actual availability, payment timing, platform rules, crypto/network fees, and outcomes can differ.

Model rules and sources
  • Every modeled campaign has three visible milestones: Step 1 purchase/start; Step 2 Day 12 clicks complete; and Step 3 Day 19 expected earnings release after the 7-day hold.
  • Optional Day-12 funds are treated as cash that is already available in the account—such as cleared new funding or referral commissions already credited to available balance. They never unlock campaign earnings early.
  • Each account may have at most 3 active campaigns. The model purchases repeatedly only while a slot and enough available balance exist.
  • First purchase at each level includes that level’s one-time activation fee; later purchases of the activated level use only campaign cost.
  • “Expected earnings” uses the published 90% figure. It excludes referrals unless explicitly entered as available cash, withdrawals, withdrawal fee, external transaction/network costs, tax, delays, missed clicks, and any additional funding not entered above.
  • Sources checked August 14, 2026: CBP Getting Started Guide, FAQ, and Terms.
